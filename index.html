01 / The beginning

Our love story

It all started in high school, at a mutual friend’s house, when two teenagers met by chance and just clicked. Neither of us knew it then, but that little moment would become the beginning of a lifetime together.

Since then, we’ve grown up side by side. Through school, university, first jobs, big dreams and little milestones, we’ve built a life we love. In 2024, we welcomed our beautiful little boy, who made our world a little fuller and our hearts a whole lot bigger.

This day is a celebration of the home we’ve built, the family we’ve created, the memories we’ve made, and all the moments still waiting for us. We can’t wait to celebrate it with you.

Christopher & Tatum
